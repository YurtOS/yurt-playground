Probably not supported on this device

The Yurt playground boots a whole Linux sandbox inside the browser tab: a kernel, a filesystem, CPython and Jupyter, running on several SharedArrayBuffer-backed Web Workers. The browser side is fine: Safari has supported SharedArrayBuffer on iOS since 15.2 on cross-origin isolated pages like this one, and Chrome and Firefox for Android do too. What phones lack is the rest: a tab that can hold around a gigabyte of memory, and workers that keep running while the page is in the background.

Open this page on a desktop or laptop browser for the full experience. On a recent tablet you can try anyway; if the sandbox runs out of memory the tab reloads or the boot stalls partway, and nothing else on the device is affected.
